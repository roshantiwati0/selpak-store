Selpak Store — UI design

Ecommerce website ka poora design, development se pehle. Sirf B2C (ghar ke customer ke liye). Catalogue 2026 ke asli products aur MRP ke saath. Har screen desktop aur mobile dono me. Kisi bhi preview par click karke poora page kholo.

1. Design systemColours, typography, spacing, buttons, cards, form fields aur team ke rules. 2. Sitemap and user flowsSaare pages ka map, customer ka flow, routes aur backend ki zarurat.

3. Screens

Design me jo cheezein placeholder hain (sir se confirm karni hain)

  • Selling price: abhi har jagah MRP dikhaya hai. Discount ho to badlega.
  • Delivery charge, delivery area (pincodes) aur minimum order.
  • Selpak Professional range (dispensers, HRT/JRT rolls) B2C design me shamil nahi hai.
  • Contact page ka address, email, timing aur WhatsApp number.
  • Product ke extra photos aur description (catalogue me ek hi photo hai).
  • Selpak logo aur pack images use karne ki brand permission.
