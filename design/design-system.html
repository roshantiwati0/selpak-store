8. Rules for the team

Do

  • Mobile pehle design karo, phir desktop.
  • Har list ka empty, loading aur error state banao.
  • Price hamesha ₹1,040 format me (en-IN).
  • Product image hamesha halka blue background par, object-fit: contain.
  • Har input ka label dikhna chahiye, sirf placeholder nahi.

Don't

  • Naye colours ya font sizes mat banao; tokens use karo.
  • Yellow ko normal text ke liye mat use karo (contrast kam).
  • Fake reviews, ratings ya discount mat dikhao.
